# Corpus changelog

## v1 (2026-09-09)

Initial corpus. 77 cases (48 malicious / 29 benign).

- 56 cases extracted from prompt-firewall's own test suite (`tests/test_scanner.py`,
  `tests/test_core.py`, `tests/test_prompt_guard2.py`) and README/security.md prose:
  leetspeak (incl. `g`->`9`), base64 (standard/urlsafe/double/triple-encoded),
  zero-width/BOM/bidi insertion, full-width homoglyph substitution, truncation/windowing
  bypass, and plaintext sanity controls. See `benchmarks/extract_corpus.py`.
- 21 cases generated by applying 10 garak-derived encoding transforms (base16/hex, base32,
  ascii85, raw hex, uuencode, ROT13, atbash, morse, NATO phonetic, Unicode tag-character
  smuggling) to 2 already-established malicious trigger phrases, plus 1 garak-sourced benign
  high-perplexity payload. See `benchmarks/garak_transforms.py` and
  `benchmarks/generate_garak_cases.py`.

Scope: injection/jailbreak-detection surface only (fast-path scanner + escalation classifier).
PII/secrets/tool-validation cases are out of scope -- see `SCHEMA.md`.
